## Goal
Oscar, in his words: "i want a full report with a market research on my idea, the competition landscape, what people are building with fable that i can get inspired from ... imagine how someone from BCG or KPMG would research to answer me and help me at the stage im at right now."

His stage (D-015 and 17:01): no spend, working outside ARESA hours. Path: refine the idea, market research, a fully simulated demo in a virtual environment as the proof of concept (built with frontier models such as Fable 5.2), pitch deck, then angel money to build the first unit or VC to start the company. He quits ARESA only once an investor is interested.

Write it the way a strategy consultant would: start from the question, build a hypothesis tree, size the market bottom up and top down (every estimate labelled as one, with its assumptions), segment the buyers, map the competition, and finish with a recommendation and next steps.

## Done when
`ventures/pocket-factory/report.md` exists with:
1. Executive summary on one page: the answer first, then three to five key findings, then the recommendation.
2. The question and the hypothesis tree: what must be true for Pocket Factory to be fundable at pre-seed.
3. Market: which segments exist (expeditionary and defence fabrication, maritime and offshore, remote and off-grid sites, repair and spare parts, education and makerspaces, space), who pays today and how much, and market size, each figure sourced or labelled as an estimate.
4. Competition landscape: a 2x2 or positioning map, plus a table of players (offer, price, funding, traction, gap). MicroFactory goes first and in depth, using `microfactory-catalogue.md`.
5. Technology readiness: what frontier models plus simulation can and cannot do today (sim-to-real), using `sim-demos-scan.md`.
6. Inspiration: what people are building with Fable and in simulation, and what Oscar can borrow for the demo.
7. Investor lens: how angels and VCs treat sim-only hardware proofs of concept, recent comparable pre-seed and seed rounds in robotics and manufacturing, sourced.
8. Recommendation for this stage: a sharp first wedge, the demo spec, what the deck must prove, and the risks, including the ARESA contract, a solo non-technical founder, and the capital intensity.
9. Next 30 days, as no-spend steps.
Every fact has a URL and every opinion is labelled. No em dashes. Decisions are named by title, never by a bare code. Use the be-ambitious skill.
