02 · Problem and why now

The problem: home objects are either well made and mute, or capable and anonymous

The problem
  • 52% of do-it-yourself smart home users report setup or connectivity problems.1
  • 55% of surveyed Brits hold at least three unused devices, and 37.9% keep old tech in a drawer.2* The world produced 62 million tonnes of e-waste in 2022; 22.3% was documented as recycled.3

What we do not claim. Design is not why people buy smart-home gear: in one 2023 survey, aesthetic design ranked last of the features tested (sample not stated).4 Our bet is narrower: design wins where people shop for decor, and the smart layer is an option, not the reason to buy.

Why now
Also in place

A cheap, open software layer. MCP has over 10,000 public servers under Linux Foundation governance;8 Gemma 3n runs in 2 to 3 GB;9 Espressif has shipped over 1.5 billion Wi-Fi chips.10

Pre-orders work for design objects. Peak Design's Roller Pro raised US$12.9M from 23,461 backers on Kickstarter in 2025.11

Import rules changed. The EUR 150 duty exemption ended 1 Jul 2026; EUR 3 per item applies to small parcels.12

09 · Business model

Business model: a premium-brand margin on a family of objects, pre-orders before the build, software only where it helps

Unit economics
Two routes, and the cost of an order

Ad cost per order, our arithmetic. A click costs about US$0.60 (Meta, all industries, 2026)84 and home and furniture sites convert about 1.29% of visits.85 That is roughly US$47 of ads per order, against EUR 82.6 kept on a EUR 100 direct sale (currencies not converted). Online orders come back 19.3% of the time.86

So (opinion): direct sales need a price premium and repeat purchases. A family of objects that look right together can raise the basket without raising the ad cost.

Evidence for the model
  • Object plus repeat purchase. Tonies, a design-led object brand: EUR 630M revenue in 2025, 62.8% gross margin, 71% of revenue from the add-on figurines, not the box.81
  • Optional software tier. The category pays US$79 a year (Skylight Plus)87 or EUR 39.90 (reMarkable Connect).88
  • Pre-orders. Roller Pro: US$12.9M, 23,461 backers.11 But more than three quarters of crowdfunded hardware ships late.89*
  • Build. ODM MOQ commonly 500 to 5,000 units (reported);90* CE, RED and FCC with a pre-certified module: US$3,000 to 8,000.91
  • Freight. About US$0.39 per boxed unit by full container (our arithmetic, assumed box size); small brands pay far more.92*
