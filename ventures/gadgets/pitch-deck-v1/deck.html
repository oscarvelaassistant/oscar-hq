02 · Problem and why now

The problem, and why the answer is possible now

The problem

Every connected object does what its maker's app allows. The owner cannot reshape it.

  • 52% of do-it-yourself smart home users report setup or connectivity problems.40
  • The biggest home assistant lost $25bn in five years and became, in a former insider's words, "a smart timer". Its AI upgrade now costs $19.99 a month.41, 42
  • The first AI gadgets were phones in disguise. Humane shipped about 10,000 units on more than $230M and is dead; Rabbit sold 100,000 and keeps 5,000 daily users; Friend sold 3,000.17, 13, 18
  • Half of US smartphone owners will not pay extra for bolt-on AI features (reported survey).43

Our reading (opinion). AI sells when it is the product inside a calm, single-purpose object that the owner can reshape by talking to it. Not another screen with a feature tax.

Why now
Also in place

Open tool standard. The Model Context Protocol, launched Nov 2024, now has more than 10,000 public servers and Linux Foundation governance with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Microsoft at the table.49, 50

Models that fit in an object. Gemma 3n runs in 2 to 3 GB of memory on phone-class hardware.48

Commodity radios. Espressif has shipped more than 1.5 billion Wi-Fi SoCs.51
