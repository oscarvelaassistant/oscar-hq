# Venture: crowd-control tech (France)

Working folder for the live venture. The idea itself, Oscar's objectives and the council of sages live on his Mac in `~/Claude Projects/InnovationHub/` and in his vault; this folder holds the company's work product so HQ runs never depend on the Mac.

## The idea, as Oscar wrote it (2026-10-18, verbatim summary sent 2026-10-23)
Policing and crowd-control tech for France. French riot police are often outnumbered, and riots spill over into looting and arson. The goal is non-lethal products that give a small police force more control and reach without adding weapons. Inspiration: the water cannons used in the South China Sea, which apply force without firing a shot.

Product directions:
- Rapid-deploy barrier vehicles that seal off streets and protect storefronts.
- Fire-suppression robots or drones for burning cars and bins.
- Tethered overwatch drones that give live crowd maps and spot breakaway groups early.
- Evidence capture for later prosecution (the EU AI Act limits real-time facial recognition, so footage is reviewed afterwards).
- Small unmanned vehicles that spray water or foam.

Risks he named: proportionality law, public backlash (the LBD rubber-bullet controversy), the politics of public procurement. His next step: talk to CRS and Gendarmerie officers about what breaks down on riot nights.
