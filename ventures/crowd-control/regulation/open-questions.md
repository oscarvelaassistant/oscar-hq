# Open questions (regulation and procurement)

What the primary sources did not settle this run, and who could. Numbered for reference from legal-map.md.

- Q1 (sharpened 2026-11-01). R. 311-2 read on Légifrance: category A2 ("armes relevant des matériels de guerre") lists "Véhicules de combat blindés ou non blindés" (8°). Open: is an armoured street-sealing vehicle with no weapon a "véhicule de combat", and does code de la défense L. 2332-1 (manufacturing and trade authorisation) follow from that? Who: the ministry's weapons classification desk (SCAE) or a public-law lawyer.
- Q2. Does mounting a water projector on an unmanned carrier change its status under R. 211-16 and D. 211-17? Who: same as Q1; also an officer interview (who operates the water cannon today and under whose order).
- Q3. Is foam a listed means? The ministry's answer names "engins lanceurs d'eau" only. Who: D. 211-17 full text on Légifrance; a CRS or EGM commander.
- Q4 (closed 2026-11-01). Article 5(1) points (ba) and (bb), applicable from 2 December 2026, prohibit AI systems that generate or manipulate intimate images of identifiable persons without consent and child sexual abuse material (Directive 2011/93/EU), read on the Commission Article 5 page marked NEW. Nothing for this venture. The EUR-Lex consolidated text itself returned only a page shell to the fetch tool twice on 2026-11-01; the Commission page states it reproduces the EUR-Lex version as at 27 July 2026.
- Q5. Which service inside the ministry buys riot-police equipment today, and its current name (SAELSI in 2020; later reorganised)? Who: the ministry organigramme; a procurement officer.
- Q6. When did the ministry last buy water cannon vehicles, from whom, and through which notice? No notice found this run. Who: BOAMP and TED searches by CPV; a CRS logistics officer.
- Q7 (updated 2026-11-01). What did the Conseil constitutionnel decide on L. 242-1 to L. 242-8 (décision 2021-834 DC, 20 January 2022), and is the seven-day retention the text in force? The consolidation shows the articles came from loi 2021-646 (art. 47) and were rewritten by article 15 of the 2022 law, with "trente jours" replaced by "sept jours". Who: next run, the decision on conseil-constitutionnel.fr and L. 242-4 on Légifrance, both reached through search-result links (Légifrance opened that way for R. 311-1 and R. 311-2 this run).
- Q8. What does the December 2021 revised SNMO say about encirclement and about new equipment? Who: next run; the text is on interieur.gouv.fr.
- Q9. Does the BSPP buy its own fire-fighting robots, and through which notices? Who: BOAMP search "BSPP" or "Préfecture de police" by CPV 34144210 and 35000000.
- Q10. Does the UGAP catalogue carry maintien de l'ordre equipment, and how is a new product referenced there? Who: ugap.fr; a procurement officer.
- Q11 (narrowed 2026-11-01). The young-enterprises sentence now has a source: the "Historique" section of the R. 2122-9-1 page says the loi de finances pour 2024 modified L. 2172-3 and names article 44 sexies-0 A CGI; the wording of L. 2172-3 itself is unread. The R. 2122-9-1 page carries no cap on the share of the total value that innovation lots may represent; the lots must meet "la condition prévue au b du 2° de l'article R. 2123-1", unread. Who: next run, L. 2172-3 and R. 2123-1 on Légifrance through search-result links.
- Q12 (added 2026-11-01). Does CSI R. 311-1's definition of "arme" ("conçu ou destiné par nature à tuer, blesser") catch a remote water or foam projector, and under which category of R. 311-2 would it fall if so? Who: the same desk or lawyer as Q1, after D. 211-17 is read (Q3).
