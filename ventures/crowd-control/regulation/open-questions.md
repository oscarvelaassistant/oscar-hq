# Open questions (regulation and procurement)

What the primary sources did not settle this run, and who could. Numbered for reference from legal-map.md.

- Q1. Does an armoured or weapon-carrying barrier vehicle fall under war-materiel classification (CSI R. 311-2 category A2; code de la défense L. 2332-1 manufacturing and trade authorisation)? Who: the ministry's weapons classification desk (SCAE) or a public-law lawyer; text to read: R. 311-2 on Légifrance (refused the fetch this run).
- Q2. Does mounting a water projector on an unmanned carrier change its status under R. 211-16 and D. 211-17? Who: same as Q1; also an officer interview (who operates the water cannon today and under whose order).
- Q3. Is foam a listed means? The ministry's answer names "engins lanceurs d'eau" only. Who: D. 211-17 full text on Légifrance; a CRS or EGM commander.
- Q4 (narrowed 2026-10-31, second evening run). Article 113 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 names the two additional prohibitions: Article 5(1) points (ba) and (bb), with Article 5(1a) and (1b), applicable from 2 December 2026. Their content was not read. Who: next run, from the EUR-Lex consolidated text (reach it through a search-result link; the 2024 OJ URL predates the amendment) or the Commission service desk Article 5 page, which marks amended passages.
- Q5. Which service inside the ministry buys riot-police equipment today, and its current name (SAELSI in 2020; later reorganised)? Who: the ministry organigramme; a procurement officer.
- Q6. When did the ministry last buy water cannon vehicles, from whom, and through which notice? No notice found this run. Who: BOAMP and TED searches by CPV; a CRS logistics officer.
- Q7. What did the Conseil constitutionnel decide on L. 242-1 to L. 242-8 (décision 2021-834 DC, 20 January 2022), and is the seven-day retention the text in force? Who: next run, from conseil-constitutionnel.fr and Légifrance.
- Q8. What does the December 2021 revised SNMO say about encirclement and about new equipment? Who: next run; the text is on interieur.gouv.fr.
- Q9. Does the BSPP buy its own fire-fighting robots, and through which notices? Who: BOAMP search "BSPP" or "Préfecture de police" by CPV 34144210 and 35000000.
- Q10. Does the UGAP catalogue carry maintien de l'ordre equipment, and how is a new product referenced there? Who: ugap.fr; a procurement officer.
- Q11 (added 2026-10-31, second evening run, from the judge's reading of the file). Code de la commande publique R. 2122-9-1 and L. 2172-3: does the lot exemption carry a cap on the share of the total value that such lots may represent, and does the sentence on young enterprises (article 44 sexies-0 A CGI) since the loi de finances pour 2024 have a text behind it? Who: next run, Légifrance or code-commande-publique.com reached through a search-result link; the falsification judge, once its fetches work.
