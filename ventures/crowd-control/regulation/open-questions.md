# Open questions (regulation and procurement)

What the primary sources did not settle, and who could. Numbered for reference from legal-map.md. Updated 2026-11-02 morning run (Q5, Q8 after Vela's BOAMP reading).

- Q1 (sharpened 2026-11-01). R. 311-2 read on Légifrance: category A2 ("armes relevant des matériels de guerre") lists "Véhicules de combat blindés ou non blindés" (8°). Open: is an armoured street-sealing vehicle with no weapon a "véhicule de combat", and does code de la défense L. 2332-1 (manufacturing and trade authorisation) follow from that? Who: the ministry's weapons classification desk (SCAE) or a public-law lawyer.
- Q2 (reframed 2026-11-01 evening). D. 211-17 holds no water device, so an unmanned carrier does not change a D. 211-17 status that does not exist; the question merges into Q12 (is a remote projector an "arme" under R. 311-1) plus the doctrine question: under whose order a water device is used today and whether the SNMO names it. Who: a CRS or EGM commander (the interview), then the SCAE or a lawyer.
- Q3 (closed 2026-11-01 evening). D. 211-17 (in force since 18 October 2021) lists grenades and 56 mm and 40 mm launchers only; R. 211-16 names grenades "principalement à effet de souffle" and their launchers. Neither water nor foam appears; the list is a firearms list, not a list of all means. See legal-map.md, second pass.
- Q4 (closed 2026-11-01). Article 5(1) points (ba) and (bb), applicable from 2 December 2026, prohibit AI systems that generate or manipulate intimate images of identifiable persons without consent and child sexual abuse material (Directive 2011/93/EU), read on the Commission Article 5 page marked NEW. Nothing for this venture.
- Q5 (narrowed again 2026-11-02).
