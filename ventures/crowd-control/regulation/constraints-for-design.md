# Constraints for design (second pass, 2026-11-01 evening; sources in legal-map.md)

1. The exhaustive weapons list of R. 211-16 and D. 211-17 (in force since 18 October 2021) holds grenades and their 56 mm and 40 mm launchers only, all category A2; water and foam projection are not on it, so a projector is governed by the general force rules (L. 211-9 sommations, R. 211-13 proportionality) and the SNMO, and the open point is whether it is an "arme" under R. 311-1 at all (Q12).
2. Any device that projects force must allow graduated, proportionate use (R. 211-13) and must be usable only after the second sommation is repeated (R. 211-11): a remote operator needs a confirm step tied to the sommations.
3. Aerial cameras, tethered included (L. 242-1 "aéronefs captifs"), may not capture sound and may not run facial recognition or any automated matching with other files (L. 242-4), and the images may not be analysed by a facial recognition system off the device either (décision 2021-834 DC §30).
4. Two deletion clocks: seven days after the deployment for aerial images (L. 242-4), 48 hours for any sequence that caught the inside of a home or its entrance when recording could not be stopped (§29); recording may not be permanent; design storage to expire, mask and flag.
5. Every drone deployment runs under a préfet's written authorisation that states the purpose, the strictly necessary perimeter and the maximum number of cameras in the air at once, granted only if no less intrusive means exists (L. 242-5, §27): the product must log where it flew, when, how many cameras, under which authorisation.
6. No real-time remote biometric identification function, ever (AI Act Article 5(1)(h)); no post identification function either unless built as a high-risk AI system under Article 26(10) and Annex III, which is a different company.
7. A street closure used to encircle needs, by doctrine (SNMO December 2021 point 3.1.4, upheld by CE n° 461513), a grave-and-imminent-violence case, a strictly necessary duration and a controlled exit point by default: a barrier product should make the exit a built-in, fast-opening feature.
8. If a vehicle is armoured or carries a listed weapon, check war-materiel classification before any drawing: CSI R. 311-2 category A2 lists "Véhicules de combat blindés ou non blindés" and "grenades, engins incendiaires" (Q1, Q12).
9. Fire-suppression devices are sécurité civile equipment: design to SDIS and BSPP needs, not police needs, and to their buying cadence.
10. Every logged action should be exportable in a form a judge can read: the Conseil d'État reviews these operations under faute lourde and proportionality, and the first sale route (R. 2122-9-1, Innov'Achats) needs a product that is demonstrably "nouveau ou sensiblement amélioré" and an internal sponsor who files it.
