## Three routes open to a new supplier

### Route 1: innovation purchase without competition, under 100,000 EUR HT
- Text: Code de la commande publique, article R. 2122-9-1, created by décret n° 2021-1634 of 13 December 2021 (permanent since; the 2018 scheme was an experiment). "L'acheteur peut passer un marché public sans publicité ni mise en concurrence préalables" "portant sur des travaux, fournitures ou services innovants au sens du second alinéa de l'article L. 2172-3" "et répondant à un besoin dont la valeur estimée est inférieure à 100 000 euros hors taxes"; lots under 80,000 EUR HT for supplies and services. Since the loi de finances pour 2024, L. 2172-3 treats as innovative what young enterprises (article 44 sexies-0 A CGI) propose (this sentence is not from the cited page and has no source of its own yet; see open question Q11, and treat it as unverified until then). Also unverified: whether the lot exemption carries a cap on the share of the total value that such lots may represent (Q11).
- Past reference: none by construction; these purchases are not published as notices. Evidence is the article itself. What it means for Oscar: a first unit or a paid trial can be bought by a préfecture, a SDIS or the ministry without a tender, if the buyer wants it. The buyer still has to want it: this route needs the officer conversations first.
- Source: https://www.code-commande-publique.com/article-r2122-9-1/ (text reproduced; Légifrance refused the fetch this run).

### Route 2: open procedure framework agreements on PLACE and BOAMP (the ministry's normal shape)
- Shape: accord-cadre, open procedure, one or few lots, multi-year, published on BOAMP and PLACE, often with TED publication above threshold.
- Reference A (police drones, 2026): BOAMP 26-34600, published 3 Apr 2026, "acquisition et la maintenance de systèmes de réception des trames de signalement électronique des drones pour les unités opérationnelles du Ministère de l'Intérieur", open procedure, framework with reopening of competition (max 15 participants), estimated 8,566,666.67 EUR HT, max 21,416,666.70 EUR, 24 months renewable to 4 years, offers due 10 Apr 2026, PLACE procedure id 35dbe2db-0499-4c3b-81da-299b6ed4d0ff, internal ref MAE20260202. https://www.boamp.fr/telechargements/FILES/PDF/2026/04/26-34600.pdf
- Reference B (police drones, 2020): BOAMP 20-51423, 12 Apr 2020, "Drones", 651 drones in three lots (565 micro-drones about 1.8 M EUR HT; 66 "national capacity" about 1.58 M EUR; about 20 nano drones 175,000 EUR), offers due 19 Jun 2020. Source is press (DroneXL, 15 Apr 2020, linking https://www.boamp.fr/avis/detail/20-51423/0); the notice itself was not fetched this run. https://dronexl.co/2020/04/15/french-government-plans-buy-651-drones-for-surveillance-purposes
- Reference C (ministry framework shape, 2026): BOAMP 26-32700, results notice 30 Mar 2026, open procedure, framework without reopening, single lot, estimated 500,000 EUR HT, awarded to an SME (MC&S) at 551,327.20 EUR, two offers received, PLACE endpoint https://www.marches-publics.gouv.fr. Shows an SME winning a ministry framework with two bidders. https://www.boamp.fr/telechargements/FILES/PDF/2026/04/26-32700.pdf
- What it means: the ministry buys drones and detection systems by open framework; a new supplier enters as a bidder or as a subcontractor of a bidder. Water cannon vehicles: no notice found this run (search returned fire-service and sécurité civile notices only); open question Q6.

### Route 3: fire-service joint procurement (for the fire-suppression direction)
- Reference: TED 692909-2023 (OJ S 220/2023, 15 Nov 2023), SDIS 35 for SDIS 35, 44, 49, 50, 76 and 85, "groupement de commandes pour l'acquisition de Camions Citernes Ruraux Moyens (CCRM)", CPV 34144210, open procedure, no lots, estimated 4,760,000 EUR, awarded 3,820,010.43 EUR HT to Iturri Pompiers France (Roanne), two tenders, contract 2023-019 of 12 Oct 2023, buyer profile megalis.bretagne.bzh, prior notice 2023/S 108-339515. https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/692909-2023/pdf
- Also: BOAMP 24-93149, Ministère de l'Intérieur (DGSCGC), framework for leasing water-bomber aircraft with crews, open procedure, max 54,000,000 EUR HT, awarded to Titan Firefighting Company SL (Spain), concluded 4 Jul 2024: the sécurité civile buys through the same ministry and awards to foreign suppliers. https://www.boamp.fr/telechargements/FILES/PDF/2024/08/24-93149.pdf
- What it means: a fire-suppression robot sells to SDIS and to the BSPP, through joint procurements led by one SDIS, with two-bidder competitions being normal.
