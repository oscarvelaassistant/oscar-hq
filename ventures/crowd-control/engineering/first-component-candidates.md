## Candidate A, tethered overwatch drone: authorisation and perimeter log board
- Component: a logging board that sits between the GNSS receiver and the ground station: microcontroller (ESP32 class), GNSS input over UART (NMEA, 38400 baud, the NEO-M9N default), real-time clock, microSD. No microphone input and no image bus, by construction. It writes one record per fix (UTC time, latitude, longitude, authorisation id, number of cameras recording), raises an alert when a fix falls outside the authorised perimeter polygon, erases records seven days after the deployment-end mark, and exports the deployment register as CSV.
- Requirement: README line 11, "Tethered overwatch drones that give live crowd maps and spot breakaway groups early." The law fixes what the board must do. L. 242-4: the devices "ne peuvent ni procéder à la captation du son", may not carry "traitements automatisés de reconnaissance faciale", nor any "rapprochement, interconnexion ou mise en relation automatisé", and recordings have a "durée maximale de sept jours à compter de la fin du déploiement du dispositif" (source 1). The Interior ministry's own page says "Les données sont effacées dans un délai de 7 jours à compter de la fin du déploiement du dispositif", 48 hours for images of the inside of homes, traces kept 3 years (source 2). L. 242-5: written decision of the préfet, "périmètre géographique strictement nécessaire", "fixe le nombre maximal de caméras", "durée maximale de trois mois", and a register of each intervention sent weekly to the préfet (source 1). Constraints 3, 4, 5, 6 and 10. Trace is to the idea note and the law, not to an interview.
- Tool: Wokwi, Community tier (tooling.md verdict "Use now, Community tier (projects public)"): simulated ESP32; part `wokwi-microsd-card` (SPI, FAT16 file system created at start, part marked as in testing, source 9); part `wokwi-ds1307` (I2C address 0x68, starts at system time, firmware can write a new time, source 10); a custom chip in C using the UART API (`uart_init`, `uart_write`) to replay a scripted NMEA track (sources 11, 12). Nothing to install.
- Test: replay 600 RMC fixes at one fix per second (a test setting, not a module default) with fixes 1 to 400 inside a test polygon and 401 to 600 outside; mark deployment end at fix 600; write the RTC to end + 604,799 s and run housekeeping; write it to end + 604,801 s and run housekeeping again.
- Pass: (1) the microSD register holds 600 records, each with UTC time, latitude, longitude and authorisation id; (2) the perimeter alert fires at fix 401 and at no earlier fix; (3) all 600 records exist at end + 604,799 s and zero records not flagged "copie pour procédure" exist at end + 604,801 s; (4) the exported CSV opens as plain text with a header row; (5) the schematic has no audio input and no camera interface. Any other outcome is a fail.
- Number not sourced: the NEO-M9N default navigation rate (the datasheet states PVT 25 Hz maximum, 2.0 m CEP, 38400 baud default, source 13; the default rate sits in the integration manual's CFG-RATE section, not reached, source 14); a real authorisation perimeter (a préfecture arrêté in a recueil des actes administratifs would give one); the 48-hour home-interior rule needs image masking, which is a camera function this board cannot test.
- Manufacturer type: PCB assembler (JLCPCB or PCBWay quote pages, per tooling.md) for the board; CNC or 3D-print shop for an enclosure. The drone and tether are bought, not made, at this stage. I do not know whether tethered-drone vendors already ship such a register; not checked this run and an interview question.

## Candidate B, small unmanned spray vehicle: graduated valve driver stage with two-step confirm
- Component: a low-side PWM MOSFET driver for a 24 V DC proportional solenoid valve with a flyback diode, and the firmware that holds the valve at 0 % until two distinct confirm inputs (sommation 1 recorded, sommation 2 recorded) are logged in order, then ramps the duty in steps.
- Requirement: README line 13, "Small unmanned vehicles that spray water or foam." and line 6, "the water cannons used in the South China Sea, which apply force without firing a shot." Constraint 1 (water projection is on the list; foam unverified, Q2), constraint 2 (graduated, proportionate use; usable only after the second sommation, so a confirm step tied to the sommations), constraint 10 (exportable log). Trace is to the idea note.
- Tool: KiCad 10.0.8 with its integrated ngspice (tooling.md "Use now", Mac install step 1) as the judge of the driver stage; Wokwi Community tier for the confirm-and-ramp firmware on a simulated ESP32 with pushbuttons and the logic analyzer. tscircuit's browser SPICE is also "use now", but whether its analog simulation accepts a MOSFET model was not verified (docs fetch blocked), so it is a sketchpad here, not the judge.
- Numbers: valve Bürkert Type 2873, article 239079: 24 V DC, 9 W, orifice 2.5 mm, Kv 0.15 m3/h, maximum 5 bar, opening time at most 20 ms, media liquid and neutral gas (source 5); same type, article 271965: PWM-controlled proportional, response under 20 ms, IP65, -1 to 12 bar, orifice 1.2 mm, Kv 0.04 m3/h (source 4); the maker's page lists 24 V, orifices 0.8 to 6 mm, media temperature -10 to 90 °C, and Type 8605 PWM electronics (source 3). MOSFET Infineon IRLML6344: VDS 30 V, ID 5.0 A at 25 °C, RDS(on) at most 37 mΩ at VGS 2.5 V and 29 mΩ at VGS 4.5 V, 1.3 W at 25 °C (source 6). Diode Vishay 1N5819: VRRM 40 V, 1 A, forward voltage 0.6 V (vendor page, test current not stated, source 7).
- Test (ngspice transient): 24 V supply, coil as 64 Ω (24 V squared over 9 W, both datasheet values) in series with an inductance left blank until sourced, gate pulse 0 to 3.3 V. Pass if (1) with the gate at 3.3 V the coil current settles at 0.375 A and the drain voltage below 0.014 V (0.375 A times 37 mΩ); (2) the drain voltage peak at turn-off stays at or below 24.6 V (24 V plus 0.6 V), under the 30 V rating; (3) average MOSFET dissipation stays below 1.3 W at every duty; (4) over duties 10 % to 100 % in ten steps the mean coil current rises at every step. Firmware pass in Wokwi: PWM stays at 0 % until both confirm inputs are logged with timestamps in order; either input alone leaves it at 0 %. Anything else is a fail.
- Number not sourced: coil inductance (needed for the current rise against the 20 ms opening time and for ripple; from Bürkert on request or measured on a sample); the PWM frequency Bürkert recommends for the 2873 (in the Type 8605 supplement listed on source 3, not fetched); flow and pressure a crowd-control jet needs at this vehicle scale (interview, plus the Interior ministry's water-cannon specification); whether foam is lawful (Q2).
- Manufacturer type: PCB assembler for the driver board; the valve from a valve maker (Bürkert); a CNC machining shop for a manifold or nozzle body later.

## Candidate C, rapid-deploy barrier vehicle: exit-gate hinge
- Component: the hinge pair (two leaves and a pin) carrying the exit-gate panel of a vehicle-mounted barrier, opening from the police side, so the barrier leaves a controlled exit.
- Requirement: README line 9, "Rapid-deploy barrier vehicles that seal off streets and protect storefronts." Constraint 7 (a controlled exit for people, CE n° 444849 para 22; removable fast enough to be proportionate), constraint 8 (the panel is unarmoured; if that changes, the war-materiel check comes before any drawing), constraint 10. Trace is to the idea note.
- Tool: FreeCAD 1.1.4, FEM workbench with CalculiX 2.23 (tooling.md "Use now, Mac install", step 2: rerun the blog cantilever and confirm 0.381 mm before trusting the setup).
- Numbers: horizontal line load on protective barriers in "areas susceptible to overcrowding": 3.0 kN/m applied at 1.1 m above floor level or at the top edge if lower (Hong Kong Buildings Department, Code of Practice for Dead and Imposed Loads 2011, amendment of 2 December 2020, Table 3.13, source 15); the UK and Éire National Annex figure for C5 assembly areas is also 3.00 kN/m (manufacturer page, source 16). Steel S355J2 to EN 10025-2: minimum yield 355 MPa at thickness 16 mm or less, 345 MPa over 16 to 40 mm, tensile 470 to 630 MPa (British Steel declaration of performance, 22 January 2026, source 17).
- Test (static linear FEA): hinge pair holding a 1.0 m wide gate panel (the load's unit length), 3.0 kN horizontal load applied at 1.1 m height, panel treated as rigid, leaves fixed at the vehicle side, S355J2 plate 16 mm or less; pin diameter and leaf thickness are the design variables. Pass if the peak von Mises stress in leaves and pin is at or below 355 MPa under this load at two mesh sizes, and the top-edge deflection is recorded. Fail if either mesh shows more than 355 MPa.
- Number not sourced: gate opening width and removal time (interview or the préfecture's operational order; the nearest regulated width is the ERP "unité de passage" in the arrêté du 25 juin 1980, article CO 36, Légifrance blocked this run); the Green Guide crush-barrier load (chapter 11, paid document, source 18) and EN 13200-3:2018 Annex A loads (paid, source 19); the Eurocode partial factors (EN 1990, paid); a deflection limit.
- Manufacturer type: steel fabrication job shop (laser-cut plate, welded leaves, machined pin) or a CNC machining shop; PCBWay quotes CNC parts from a FreeCAD STEP per tooling.md.
