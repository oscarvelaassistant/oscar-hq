# Rejected ideas (creative researcher)

Task 2026-10-31-004, first sweep, 2026-10-31. Ideas examined and dropped, with the reason, so they are not re-proposed. Hypothesis numbers refer to hypotheses.md.

## Animal herding and shepherding

1. Locust marching bands. Bazazi S. et al. (2008), "Collective motion and cannibalism in locust migratory bands", Current Biology 18(10), 735-739, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2008.04.035. Motion is driven by avoiding bites from behind. The transfer is pressure on the rear of a crowd, which is the crush mechanism of H3. No safe transfer.
2. Laser bird dispersal at airports. A laser aimed at people is a dazzler, a weapon under the venture's no-added-weapons constraint. Dropped without a hypothesis.
3. Bird distress calls and pyrotechnic scarers. The effect rests on species-specific alarm calls and fades with habituation. No human analogue beyond sirens and sommations, which are standard practice. The FAA and USDA manual (Cleary and Dolbeer 2005, "Wildlife Hazard Management at Airports") was not fetched in this sweep, so no primary source either.
4. Robot Sheepdog (Vaughan et al., Oxford, ducks herded by a robot) and Grandin's flight zone and point of balance (handler walks past the shoulder in the opposite direction to make the animal move; back out of the flight zone once it moves, https://www.grandin.com/behaviour/principles/flight.zone.html). The first is the same drive-from-behind mechanism as H1. The second reads like advance-and-halt practice; not filed until an officer says their advance is continuous. Passed to customer-discovery as a question: "Do you advance continuously or in steps, and what tells you to stop a step?"

## Crowd science

1. Asymmetric column in front of the exit (Helbing, Farkas, Vicsek 2000). Human experiments did not reproduce the gain: Garcimartín A. et al. (2018), "Redefining the role of obstacles in pedestrian evacuation", New Journal of Physics, record at https://portalcientifico.unav.edu/documentos/63950a9937f90f20be7b7201. Evidence contested; dropped until it settles.
2. Counterflow lane formation. Lanes form and break down on their own at high density; the only lever is to avoid pushing a crowd into an inflow and to keep an exit open, which is doctrine and law, not a product. The Conseil d'État annulled the encirclement provision of the schéma national du maintien de l'ordre for lack of precise framing (décision n° 444849, 10 June 2021, https://www.conseil-etat.fr/fr/arianeweb/CE/analyse/2021-06-10/444849). Passed to regulation-procurement as context, not as a hypothesis.
3. Hajj time-slot scheduling and one-way routing of pilgrims (Helbing 2007, measures for 1427H). Rests on a cooperative, pre-registered crowd. No plausible transfer to a riot crowd.
4. Green Guide and Fruin level-of-service density thresholds for egress planning. Standard practice for event safety officers at licensed venues; the measurement side is covered by H3. Primary text not fetched in this sweep.

## Fluids and traffic

- Off-centre column before an exit, as a device: the 2018 evacuation drills found no change in flow rate and no fewer long clogs. Reason: primary evidence contradicts the mechanism in humans. The pace effect from the same paper was kept as H4.
- Hostile-vehicle-mitigation bollard spacing (PAS 68, IWA 14-1): mobile vehicle barriers are already deployed at French events and the standards are paid texts. Reason: already standard practice.
- Traffic chicanes (horizontal deflection) to slow a crowd rush: traffic-calming primers measure vehicle speed only. Reason: no primary source for the pedestrian case.
- Zipper merge at lane drops. Reason: no plausible transfer; police do not merge two crowd lanes into one.

## Fire and rescue

- Forest-fire aerial ignition drones (plastic sphere dispensers for backburns). Reason: illegal under the constraints and no plausible transfer.
- Thermal hotspot detection from forest-fire drones: thermal payloads are already standard on public-safety drones. Reason: already standard practice, no new mechanism.
- Mine-rescue robots (explosion-proof enclosures, tethered comms, gas sensing). Reason: no plausible transfer beyond existing police UGVs; the mine-specific features do not map to a street.
- Positive-pressure ventilation fans to push tear gas out of a street: the NIST PPV work concerns enclosed structures. Reason: no primary source for open-air use.
- Water-mist or foam curtain as a radiant-heat shield for a line facing a burning barricade: industrial water-curtain literature exists but was not fetched, because a curtain does not stop burning liquid from a Molotov and the heat problem is handled by distance. Reason: no plausible transfer.

## Policing abroad

- Dialogue police (Sweden, GODIAC model): already standard French practice. The SNMO of 16 September 2020 creates a "dispositif de liaison et d'information lors des manifestations" whose team has "pour unique fonction de faciliter le déroulement de la manifestation" (pp. 13-14), https://interieur.gouv.fr/fr/content/download/124155/994809/file/2020-09-16-Shema-National-Maintien-Ordre.pdf. No product transfer.
- Visible warnings (Hong Kong flag system, text boards for sommations): already standard French practice. SNMO p. 21 lists "haut-parleurs de forte puissance, panneaux à message variable" as the new sommation equipment. The IPCC use-of-force chapter as fetched (https://www.ipcc.gov.hk/doc/en/report/thematic_report/Police%20Use%20of%20Force%20in%20Public%20Order%20Policing.pdf, para 6.18) only states the verbal-warning rule.
- Directed-energy intermediate force (JIFCO Active Denial, dazzlers): adds a weapon, contrary to the brief, and carries the backlash risk Oscar named. Not pursued to a primary source.
- Containment as a tactic to build products around (UK practice): restricted in France. Conseil d'État, 10 June 2021, n° 444849, annulled point 3.1.4 of the SNMO (encirclement) because the scheme did not "encadrer précisément les cas dans lesquels elle peut être mise en oeuvre", https://www.conseil-etat.fr/fr/arianeweb/CE/decision/2021-06-10/444849. The regulation role can check the wording of the revised SNMO.
- Hong Kong blue-dye water for later identification: no primary source fetched in this sweep (the IPCC chapter read does not cover the water-cannon vehicle).

## Nature and games

- Ant traffic (Poissonnier et al. 2019, eLife, https://elifesciences.org/articles/48945, fetched: flow "showed no sign of declining even when occupancy reached 0.8" because "ants restrained themselves from entering a crowded path"): no transfer beyond ramp metering. People do not self-restrain at a crowded entry, so the police would have to meter the inflow, which is the bottleneck-metering result already in the fluids-and-traffic field (H5).
- Cops and robbers on planar graphs (Aigner and Fromme 1984, three cops suffice): no plausible transfer, capture in the game is one cop reaching the robber's vertex, which does not stop a group. Not fetched.
- Predator-prey evasion geometry (Howland 1974, turning radius against speed): no plausible transfer, one pursuer and one evader with fixed speed and turning radius; a crowd breakaway is many against many on a street network. Not fetched.
- Fish-school fountain manoeuvre (the school splits around the predator and re-forms behind it): no plausible transfer beyond existing line formations, a point charge splits the crowd and a line displaces it, which is already how squads move. Not fetched.
- Termite tunnel traffic: no plausible transfer found, the studies concern tunnel geometry in soil. Not fetched.
