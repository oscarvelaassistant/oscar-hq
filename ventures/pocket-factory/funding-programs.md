# Pocket Factory: programmes Oscar could apply to for money to build it

Written 2026-11-17 by the wake HQ run (task 2026-11-17-001: Oscar's 20:40 request and his 20:50 update, filed by Innovator). Research only: nothing was applied to, signed up for, sent, posted or bought. Independent of Innovator's own search. Powerhouse Residency (Shenzhen) is excluded because Oscar already has it.

**How to read it.**
- Checked date for every row: 2026-11-17. "Own page" means I read the term on the programme's own site today. "Secondary" means a press article, an aggregator or a search-result summary of the official page; re-read the official page before relying on it. "Not published" means I looked and did not find it.
- Ranking follows the 20:50 update: cash first, and relocating or leaving ARESA counts as fine. Part A holds the programmes whose published rules let him apply as he is today (solo, no company, no prototype) or do not say. Part B holds the ones that say no today, with what would unlock each. Inside each part the order is by the cash the source states. Currencies differ and I did not convert them, so treat neighbouring ranks as ties. US programmes are tagged because he put the US last.
- "Eligible now" is my reading of the published rules, not a promise from the programme.

## 3. My top 3 picks (Claude's opinion)

1. **EWOR Ideation Fellowship (4).** Reason: it is the largest sum I found that is aimed at one person before a company exists, and it is open today.
   What he needs ready: the vision sentence and a personal case for why he builds this (shipyard, reserve experience, the tools he built). A deck helps; a demo is not stated as required. No cofounder needed.
2. **An Antler residency: Canada in September 2027, or Korea in March (8, 6).** Reason: it pays a solo person with no team to find the engineer cofounder, and that missing person is what blocks HAX, TAQADAM and much of the rest.
   What he needs ready: a founder CV and the idea on one page. No deck, demo or cofounder to apply. For Korea he would need to settle the Powerhouse overlap. Visas and working language are not covered on the pages, so that is a question for Antler.
3. **Y Combinator, late application (2).** Reason: it is the largest cheque open today that accepts a solo founder with an idea, and trying costs one form.
   What he needs ready: the written application and a short founder video (YC's usual format, from my general knowledge, not re-read today). YC says most businesses need a technical cofounder, so a named engineer, even part-time, would help (my opinion).

Not a pick, but dated: Start-Up Chile closes on 24 Nov. If he applies to everything, that one goes first. I could not see its form, which sits behind a portal login.

## 4. What the search shows (facts, then my reading)

- The programmes that write cheques above 200,000 and accept a solo founder mostly add a preference for someone who builds: speedrun ("a team that's already building"), South Park Commons ("applicants who can build and prototype"), Y Combinator (technical cofounder), HAX (rejects sole founders with contracted engineers).
- Antler and Entrepreneur First are designed to supply the missing cofounder. EWOR is the one sizeable cheque that states no such preference.
- Spanish public money, except Puertos 4.0 Ideas, starts only after a company exists and other money is in.
- My reading: with salary no longer a constraint, an engineer cofounder or a working prototype is what unlocks most of this list, and Powerhouse is his first shot at the prototype.
